Happy Girlfriend's Day

Some days deserve their own name — and today's yours. Not because you need a holiday to be appreciated, but because I like any excuse to say it properly. So — a few things, out of many:

  • Your laugh — the kind that sneaks out before the joke's even finished.
  • The little things — how you remember details I didn't think anyone else noticed.
  • Your steadiness — the calm you bring on the days that feel like too much.
  • Your curiosity — always noticing, always asking, always making the world feel a little bigger.
  • Ordinary days — how a plain Tuesday turns into something worth remembering, just because you're in it.
  • Simply you — not who you think you should be. Just you. Somehow, that's everything.

Thank you for choosing me, on the easy days and the harder ones. Happy Girlfriend's Day, Coco. I love you, plainly and completely.

P.S. — you're my favourite part of every day, not just this one.

— Always, Spandan
